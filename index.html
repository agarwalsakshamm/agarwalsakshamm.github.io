03 / A BIT ABOUT METhe person behind the pull requests.

Curious about
how things work.
And how they
could work better.

I’m a software engineer who thinks in systems: how the pieces fit together, where they might fail, and what it takes to make them work reliably at scale.

My experience spans firmware, performance tooling, and large-scale product engineering. I started at Western Digital; at Microsoft, I’ve applied that foundation to Windows backup, restore, and PC migration, contributing to experiences used by millions of people.

I like tracing a problem back to its cause, making sense of the data, and building a solution that holds up beyond the happy path. Lately, I’ve also been exploring LLMs, agentic AI, and how they can make software more useful.
